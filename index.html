How to play

  1. 1

    Tap a reservoir

    A stream of color pours out and flows along the tracks in real time.

  2. 2

    Plan the order

    Junctions switch after every pour, so the order you pour in decides where each color goes.

  3. 3

    Fill every jar

    Each color must reach its own jar. Use fewer pours to earn all three stars.
