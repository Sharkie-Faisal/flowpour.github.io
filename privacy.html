Privacy Policy — FlowPour

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ideonix Studios ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game FlowPour (the "Game").

1. Information we collect ourselves

The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other personal information, and we do not run our own user accounts.

  • Game progress and settings (levels completed, coins, power-ups, sound and haptics settings) are stored on your device.
  • Cloud save (optional): if you tap Connect in Settings, your game progress is saved to your own Google Play Games account using Google's Saved Games service. We do not receive your Google account details beyond the display name shown in the Game. Google's privacy policy applies to that service.

2. Advertising

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and (where you allow it) personalize ads, AdMob may collect and process: the device advertising identifier (Advertising ID), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, OS version, app version), and interactions with ads and the app, plus diagnostics.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or certain US states, the Game asks for your consent / privacy choices before ads are personalized. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Ad choices (shown where required). You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details; we receive a confirmation of the purchase so the Game can deliver your coins.

4. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game, or with Settings → Reset progress.
  • To delete your cloud save, remove FlowPour's data from your Google Play Games account (Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data), or email us.
  • For ad data, use your device's Advertising ID settings and the choices in Settings → Ad choices.

6. Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs used in the Game is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we rely on the protections provided by the Android platform and Google services.

7. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services / Play Games — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — policies.google.com/privacy

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows when it last changed.
